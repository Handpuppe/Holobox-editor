- App hernoemd tot Holobox Zorgsimulator.

- Mediamanifest, docentkalibratie en veilige opslagmigratie van schema 1 naar 2.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-09-12

- First release of Holobox Speech Therapy – Virtual Client Simulator.
- Scenario: intake interview with fictional client Erik de Vries (aphasia after stroke).
- Local scoring, notes, conclusion, results and history.
- Holobox portrait layout 1080 × 1920 with a forced white background.
