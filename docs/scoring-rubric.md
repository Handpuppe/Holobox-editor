# Scoring rubric (v1.0.0)

Total score = round(sum of competency% × weight), clamped to 0–100.

| Competency                         | Weight | Typical evidence                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | -----: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Adapted communication              |    30% | Short sentences, one question at a time, response time, communication support |
| History taking and client goals    |    25% | Presenting concern, home communication, participation priorities              |
| Observation                        |    15% | What was heard/seen, fatigue, word-finding, no invented severity              |
| Clinical reasoning                 |    15% | Summary, first objective, next step within the SLT role                       |
| Empathy and professional behaviour |    15% | Address Erik directly, acknowledge frustration, no infantilization            |

Per scored event:

- highly appropriate = 1.0
- partially appropriate = 0.5
- inappropriate or unsafe = 0

Unanswered decision points after an early end still count in the denominator (earned 0).

Free text is educational only and does not change the number.
